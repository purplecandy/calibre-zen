# The welcome pages' recordings

One animated WebP (preferred) or GIF per step, named in `page.py`'s `STEPS`
and played by `QMovie` under the wizard's header. A step whose file is not
here yet shows a labelled frame in its place.

Record at the window's proportions, in the neutral scheme, light. Animated
WebP keeps full colour at a fraction of a GIF's size; the bundled Qt plays
both. `CALIBRE_ZEN_ONBOARDING_DEMO=/path/to/file` previews a candidate on the
first step without copying it here.
